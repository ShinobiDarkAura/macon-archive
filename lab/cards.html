Ten ways to cut a card

Ten genuinely different cards this time: different proportions, orientations, typefaces and hierarchies, not one layout in ten finishes.

Two corrections from looking at a real refill card.

The notches are on the bottom edge, not the top, and they are not bites taken out of it. They are two small tabs that hang below the bottom edge on narrow necks, and they hook over the two wire rails. Card 01 copies that geometry exactly.

And a real card is landscape, 4 by 2.25 inches. Every card I drew last time was portrait, which is why none of them read as a rolodex card. At your 286px column a true card would be only 161px tall, so the faithful ones here are drawn wider than the current marginalia. That is a real decision: a correct card wants a wider left column than the app currently gives it.
